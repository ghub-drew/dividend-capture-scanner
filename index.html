Strategy Overview

Dividend Capture Scanner

A systematic approach to collecting dividend income by timing stock purchases around ex-dividend dates.

🔍
Step 1
Find a Good Stock
  • A company announces it will pay a dividend
  • The tool automatically identifies good capture candidates
  • Key filters: dividend amount, stock price, trading volume, and how quickly the stock historically recovers after the ex-date
🛒
Step 2
Buy Before the Ex-Dividend Date
  • You buy the stock before the cutoff date to qualify for the dividend
📅
Step 3
Ex-Dividend Date Passes
  • You officially receive the dividend
  • The stock price typically drops around this time
💰
Step 4
Sell the Stock
  • You sell once the price recovers
If the dividend received is more than the price drop, you profit
📊
Step 5
Track the Result
  • Record the trade: what was bought, when, how much capital deployed, and the outcome

Why Yahoo Finance and not Dividend.com?
Dividend.com has no public API. Their data is only accessible through their website, and scraping it is fragile and against most sites' terms of service. Yahoo Finance provides the same dividend calendar data cleanly through the yfinance library, making it the better starting point.
Why is IBKR not connected yet?
IBKR does have an API (Client Portal API and TWS API), but we are waiting for API access to be enabled on the account. Once credentials are shared, the scanner can check live bid/ask spreads, confirm liquidity before a trade, and optionally place orders directly from the shortlist.
